## 1. Change Management Context & Objective

Process optimization initiatives in banking fail most frequently not due to technological shortcomings, but due to **human friction, organizational inertia, and inadequate change enablement**. 

Transitioning NovaBank from a manual, paper-heavy, email-driven origination process to an automated, exception-based digital operating model impacts over 350 employees across 45 branches, central operations, credit risk, and compliance teams.

The Change Management strategy applies the **ADKAR framework (Awareness, Desire, Knowledge, Ability, Reinforcement)** to guarantee operational readiness and sustained adoption.

## 2. Stakeholder Impact & Role Transition Matrix

| Stakeholder Role | AS-IS Operational Burden | TO-BE Evolved Role & Focus | Key Resistance Factors | Change Intervention & Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Relationship Managers (Sales)** | Spends 40% of working day chasing missing customer documents and answering status calls. | Re-focused on client advisory, cross-selling wealth products, and high-value loan conversions. | Fear of technical complexity; skepticism that digital checklist will capture all edge cases. | • Mobile tablet lead intake training.<br>• Demonstration of instant pre-qualification tool reducing customer drop-off. |
| **Branch Operations Officers** | Repetitive manual keying across 4 screens; dealing with frustrated branch walk-in applicants. | Focus on high-touch customer onboarding and assisted digital document scanning. | Fear that automation will render branch operations roles redundant. | • Repositioning branch officers as "Digital Ambassadors".<br>• Hands-on scanner barcode training. |
| **Loan Operations Specialists** | Sifting through thousands of un-indexed PDFs; manually compiling files via email. | Managing exception queues, monitoring data validation anomalies, and reviewing OCR edge cases. | Reluctance to abandon legacy paper checklists; perceived loss of process ownership. | • Interactive walkthroughs of the new Unified Operations Queue.<br>• Clear operational SOPs for exception handling. |
| **Credit Underwriters** | Reviewing mundane low-risk files in a first-in, first-out queue; constant backlog. | Dedicated risk specialists focusing on high-ticket exposures, complex financials, and policy waivers. | Fear that automated Straight-Through-Processing (STP) will approve bad credit risks. | • Joint risk calibration workshops demonstrating that STP rules mirror credit policy exactly.<br>• Underwriter veto authority on edge cases. |
| **KYC / Compliance Analysts** | Manually re-typing national IDs into external government registries and screening portals. | Specialized AML investigators analyzing true positive sanction hits and complex ownership structures. | Concern over regulatory liability if automated API screening experiences outages. | • Automated fallback protocols.<br>• Comprehensive audit trail logging for regulatory reporting. |

## 3. Change Enablement Workstreams

### Workstream 1: Communication & Awareness Campaign
- **Executive Sponsorship Townhalls:** Bi-weekly video broadcasts led by Head of Retail Banking explaining the business urgency (customer retention, competitive parity).
- **"Day in the Life" Vignettes:** Short video demonstrations showing how underwriters, branch staff, and RMs save 2+ hours daily.

### Workstream 2: Role-Based Training & Certification Program
- **Module A: Branch Digital Intake:** 4-hour hands-on workshop on mobile portal navigation, document scanning, and resolving pre-validation flags.
- **Module B: Underwriter Decision Workbench:** 6-hour simulation training on reviewing OCR outputs, assessing DTI deviations, and recording Adverse Action codes.
- **Super-User Network:** 2 trained "Digital Champions" appointed in every branch to provide peer support during live rollout.

### Workstream 3: Operational Readiness & Transition Support
- **Cutover Dry Runs:** End-to-end simulated loan processing across 5 pilot branches 3 weeks prior to regional go-live.
- **Hypercare Command Center:** Dedicated 24/7 technical and operational support desk active for 60 days post-launch to resolve user questions within 15 minutes.
